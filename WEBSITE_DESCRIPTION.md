## 🌟 Executive Summary

**Artimas 2026** is the flagship annual national technological and cultural festival celebrating the timeless convergence of ancient Indian Vedic mythology and cutting-edge engineering innovation. 

Rather than a conventional techfest, Artimas presents an **interactive, cinematic 3D digital realm**. Centered around the grand **Chakra of Time (Kala Chakra)**, participants traverse four distinct cosmic epochs (*Satya Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dwapara Yuga, and Kali Yuga*), testing their intellect, architectural prowess, cybersecurity intuition, and creative grit across 8 royal trial decrees.

### 1. 🟡 Satya Yuga — The Age of Truth & Pure Intellect
*The Golden Age of virtue, wisdom, and cosmic equilibrium.*
* **Datathon (Matsya Golden Fish)**: A data science and predictive machine learning arena challenging participants to uncover truth from massive datasets.
* **Surprise Event (Kurma Golden Tortoise)**: Capture the moment through your lens! Showcase your photography skills and artistic vision in this competitive photography event. Express your creativity and tell stories through stunning images.

---

### 2. 🟠 Treta Yuga — The Age of Valor & Righteous Action
*The Epoch of devotion, sharp strategy, and sacred duty.*
* **Prompt Relay (Golden Lotus)**: A high-velocity generative AI relay race testing prompt engineering precision, LLM steering, and creative problem resolution.
* **Brandathon (Golden Rath)**: A strategic brand design, visual identity, and product positioning trial testing marketing acumen and mythic storytelling.

---

### 3. 🟣 Dwapara Yuga — The Age of Strategy & Architectural Mastery
*The Epoch of complex strategy, epic conflict, and mastery over skill.*
* **HackMatrix (Golden Emblem)**: A 24-hour overnight hackathon demanding full-stack architectural engineering, rapid prototyping, and scalable system deployment.
* **Capture the Flag (Golden Peacock Feather)**: A cybersecurity proving ground spanning reverse engineering, web security, cryptography, binary exploitation, and digital forensics.

---

### 4. 🔴 Kali Yuga — The Age of Discord & Digital Chaos
*The Iron Age of turbulence, shadow algorithms, and high-speed wit.*
* **Among Us (Golden Cosmic Blade)**: A physical and digital social deduction arena testing psychological deception, deduction defense, and team survival.
* **Houdini Heist (Golden Gada Mace)**: A rapid cipher escape room challenge requiring algorithmic decryption, puzzle solving, and rapid breakout execution.

## 🎨 Design System & Aesthetic Language

1. **Color Palette & Royal Materials**:
   - **Obsidian Dark Void (`#080A0C`)**: Deep cosmic background ensuring rich depth.
   - **Antiqued Gold & Bronze (`#C9A45C`, `#76552F`, `#B8945A`)**: Emulating ancient gilded inscriptions and royal decree seals.
   - **Ivory Glow (`#E8D8B0`)**: Crisp, readable heading typography.
   - **Aged Parchment Panels**: Radial gradients paired with ornamental drop shadows and subtle inner glows.

2. **Typography**:
   - **Headings & Titles**: `Cinzel Decorative` (Google Fonts) — Regal, ancient, and mythic.
   - **Body & Lore**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Google Fonts) — Elegant, literary, and evocative.

3. **Interactive 3D & Cinematic Motion**:
   - **3D Chakra Medallion**: Fully interactive WebGL/glTF 3D component rendered with `@google/model-viewer` that spins and rises from the depths.
   - **Seamless Parallax Depth**: Multi-layer video backgrounds optimized with Cloudinary `f_auto,q_auto` codecs for instant streaming.
   - **Royal Coverflow Carousel**: 3D perspective scroll system for event decree browsing on desktop and touch-swipe mobile.

## 🚀 Key Website Features & Architecture

* **Interactive Yugas Navigator**: Rotate the central wheel to transition background video worlds and rise-up event showcase cards in real-time.
* **Event Decree Deck**: 3D Coverflow stage with mythic center artwork, comprehensive rulebooks, and streamlined multi-step team registration wizards.
* **Responsive Multi-Tier Layouts**:
  - Desktop: Full 3D perspective Coverflow with floating navigation islands.
  - Mobile & Tablet: Ultra-compact frosted glass capsule pill navbar, touch swipe pan-y gestures, and tap-highlight-free interactions.
* **Performance & SEO Ready**: Next.js 16 App Router, Turbopack, automatic font optimization, and zero layout shift.
